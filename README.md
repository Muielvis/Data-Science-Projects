# Aviation Accident Analysis

Thank you for going through my Github repository.

This project focuses around analysing Aviation data from 1983 onwards with a key emphasis on knowing which aircraft makes are expensive to insure.

The major factors that affect aircraft insuarance include:
- Make and model.
- Total injuries caused by an aviation accident.
- Who has built the aircraft in question.
- Engine type and number of engines on the aircraft.

The major takeaways I was able to get from the data are:
1. Boeing is the most expensive aircraft to insure as its toal fatal and serious injuries are higher than aviation standards(***Total Fatal injuries mean is at 0.65 and Total Serious injuries mean is 0.28***).
2. A majority of Large airplanes use a 2-engine configuration.
3. Both large and small aircrafts have a reciprocating engine type.
4. The most popular purpose of flight was for personal reasons therefore implying that most people either used commercial flights or owned their own aircraft.
